# Interview Preparation

This folder contains all materials you create to prepare for this opportunity.

## What to Include

**Interview questions** — Questions you expect based on the job description or company style. Practice your answers.

**Company research** — Key facts about the company, recent news, product roadmap, competitive landscape.

**Technical preparation** — If this is a technical interview, practice problems, code samples, or study notes.

**Stories and examples** — Concrete stories from your experiences that demonstrate skills the company values. Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

**Questions to ask them** — Prepare thoughtful questions to ask your interviewers about the role, team, and company. This shows genuine interest.

**Practice notes** — After mock interviews or interviews with friends, save feedback here.

## Organization

You can structure this folder however works best for you:

- Separate files for each interview round (e.g., 01-phone-screen-prep.md, 02-on-site-prep.md)
- Category folders (technical-prep/, behavioral-prep/, company-research/)
- One comprehensive prep document
- Or any structure that helps you stay organized

The goal is to have preparation materials ready when you need them.
