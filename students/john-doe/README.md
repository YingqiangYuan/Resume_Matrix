# Your Career Profile

This folder contains your professional identity, experiences, and job-targeted materials. It serves as the source of truth for all career information that gets customized for different opportunities.

## Files

- **[objective.md](./objective.md)** — Your career goals, target job families, target industries, graduation timeline, and visa requirements

- **[narrative.md](./narrative.md)** — Personal statement about who you are, your professional philosophy, and what makes you different

- **[resume.md](./resume.md)** — Master resume template, the foundation for all role-specific versions

- **[resume-role-1.md](./resume-role-1.md)** — Resume customized for [Job Family 1]

- **[resume-role-2.md](./resume-role-2.md)** — Resume customized for [Job Family 2]

- **[resume-role-3.md](./resume-role-3.md)** — Resume customized for [Job Family 3]

## Folders

- **[experiences/](./experiences/README.md)** — Your work and project history, organized by role or project

## How to Use

When you apply for a new opportunity, refer to the resume that matches the job family. If you need to fine-tune it further for a specific company, save the customized version in the corresponding interview folder.

As you gain new experiences, add them to the experiences folder. The narrative and role-specific resumes can then be updated to emphasize the most relevant achievements.
