## About Pulse Social

Pulse Social is a consumer social product. We have about 8 million monthly active users, 200 employees, and a tight engineering culture. We're best known for a home feed that people open dozens of times per day. Most of our product investment goes into making that home feed feel alive, relevant, and worth the next scroll.

We're not Meta-scale and we don't want to be. The bet is that a smaller product, run well, can hold a defensible niche in consumer social by treating the feed as a craft.

---

## Purpose

As a Backend Engineer Intern on the Platform Engineering team, you will own one focused project for the summer with concrete production impact. Reporting to a Senior Backend Engineer who specializes in feed infrastructure, you will be expected to ship code that lives in the production path by the end of your internship, not a side project that gets thrown away.

This is a New Grad / Internship opening targeted at students currently in a Master's or final year of Bachelor's CS or related programs. We do not expect you to arrive with deep distributed systems experience. We do expect you to write clean Go (or equivalent in one other systems language and demonstrate you can ramp on Go quickly), to know SQL beyond the basics, and to be willing to learn the rest on the job from the people next to you.

## Accountability

- Take ownership of one focused backend project for the 12-week internship, scoped together with your mentor in the first two weeks.
- Write production-grade Go code with appropriate testing, observability, and code review discipline.

- Operate the service you ship including basic on-call participation in the last two weeks.
